# Reel 01 — "One word, repeated"

First post on @monkmanthra. 0 followers, 0 posts, no licence yet, nothing
buyable that we want traffic on. So this reel's job is **not** to sell. It
introduces the idea and the name, and earns the right to post a product later.

- **28s**, 9:16, 1080x1920
- 4 Flow clips at 8s, trimmed to 32s of footage → 28s cut
- Female VO (Kokoro), music bed, no on-screen hype

## Why this and not a product launch

The brand guide is motion-averse on purpose — "never loops, never pulses",
"earn the calm". A launch reel fights that. The idea page already has the
strongest opening line the brand owns, and it is not an ad:

> A manthra is one word, repeated, until it changes something.

Making that the first thing the account ever says sets the register for
everything after it. It also makes **no health claim**, which matters: there
is no FSSAI licence yet and the site carries a regulator disclaimer.

---

## Voiceover — 55 words, ~24s at Kokoro's pace

    A manthra is one word.
    Repeated.
    Until it changes something.

    That is also how a body works.
    A small dose, repeated, at the same hour.

    This is Golden Milk.
    Turmeric, ginger, cinnamon.
    Five grams, in the evening.
    The warm milk is up to you.

    Monk Manthra. Coming soon.

Line breaks are **render units, not formatting** — generate per line and
place them on the timeline, so a slow line can be given room without
re-rendering the whole take. Lines 1-3 want real silence between them; the
sentence only works if it is not rushed.

Last two words carry the whole call to action. There is no link and no
"tap here", because there is nothing ready to tap.

## Shots

Palette for every prompt: deep purple `#3A1F5C`, gold `#C2A053`,
half-white `#F4F1EC`. No other colour enters the frame.

### Clip 1 — 0:00-0:07 · over VO lines 1-3

    Extreme macro, a single drop of warm golden liquid falling into still
    milk in a dark ceramic bowl, shot from directly above. One slow
    concentric ripple travels outward from the point of impact and fades.
    Deep purple shadows, warm gold highlight, cream white liquid. Single
    soft window light from the left. Shallow depth of field. Static camera,
    no movement. Slow motion. Photographic, not stylised.

Why: the ripple **is** the mark — a seed at the centre, rings travelling
outward. The logo doesn't need to appear for the idea to land, and a real
ripple earns it in a way an animated logo would not.

### Clip 2 — 0:07-0:14 · over VO lines 4-5

    Macro tabletop, a small brass spoon lifting fine golden turmeric powder
    from a matte white ceramic dish. A thin stream of powder falls back into
    the dish. Warm side light, deep purple background falling into shadow.
    Static camera. Slow, unhurried. Real texture, visible grain of the
    powder. Photographic.

### Clip 3 — 0:14-0:21 · over VO lines 6-9

    Macro, three small ceramic dishes in a row on dark stone — golden
    turmeric powder, fresh ginger root, two cinnamon quills. Slow push in
    along the row. Deep purple background, single warm light from the
    upper left, deep shadows. Calm, still, no hands. Photographic.

If Flow struggles with three dishes, shoot them as three separate 3s clips
and cut on the VO's own commas. That is cheaper than fighting the prompt.

### Clip 4 — 0:21-0:28 · over VO lines 10-11

    A matte deep purple stand-up pouch on a dark stone surface, warm side
    light, deep shadow behind. Camera perfectly static. Steam drifts slowly
    across the frame from the left. Nothing else moves. Photographic,
    product photography, generous empty space around the pouch.

**The pouch label will be wrong.** Flow cannot render the wordmark or the
mark correctly and will invent something close and wrong — which is worse
than blank. Two options: prompt an unlabelled pouch and composite the real
artwork on in post, or hold the last 4s on a plain purple card carrying the
real mark. The second is cheaper and more on-brand.

## Captions

Restrained, and this is a deliberate break from the house style used on the
other channels. The big one-word-at-a-time centre caption is a retention
device for fast formats; on this brand it would be exactly the "nag" the
guide names. Instead:

- Lower third, half-white on nothing, ~34px
- One full phrase at a time, matching a VO line
- Fades in, holds, fades out. No pop, no bounce, no per-word reveal

Accessibility is the real reason to caption at all here — most of Instagram
watches muted, and a silent viewer should still get the sentence.

## Music

One sustained low drone or a single tanpura note. No percussion — a beat
implies a pace, and the whole line is about not hurrying. Duck it under the
VO by about 12dB.
