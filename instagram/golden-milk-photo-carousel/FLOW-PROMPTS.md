# Golden Milk — photo carousel

Same visual system as `../monk-manthra-photo-carousel/` (that carousel is
paused for now — this one's the priority), same locked motif: turmeric
powder meeting milk, no packets, no hands/models. This carousel's copy is
specifically about the Golden Milk product instead of the brand in
general, pulled from the product page's own language
([products/golden-milk.html](../../products/golden-milk.html)) rather
than invented.

**You can reuse the exact same 6 photos from the other carousel if you
already generated them** — the motif and prompts are identical, only the
text overlay differs. No need to regenerate in Flow unless you want
different shots for variety.

7 slides. 6 photos + 1 pure-gradient closer (already built, no photo
needed).

## Prompt scaffold (identical to the brand carousel)

> Macro photograph, shallow depth of field, dramatic single-source
> lighting from the upper left, deep shadow falling away into near-black.
> Subject: turmeric powder (vivid golden-yellow) and dairy milk (warm
> white), nothing else in frame. Matte surfaces, no gloss, no plastic, no
> packaging of any kind, no labels, no text, no logos, no hands, no
> models, no props, no plants, no scattered spices beyond the turmeric
> itself. Photographic, not a 3D render or illustration. Square, 1:1,
> high detail, editorial food-photography quality. Background is
> near-black or deep aubergine-purple, letting the gold and white read as
> the only bright colours in frame.

**Negative prompt:** hands, fingers, model, face, packaging, pouch, jar,
label, text, watermark, signature, logo, gloss, plastic, gradient
background, lens flare, multiple light sources, busy background, wood
grain, marble, props, spoons other than where specified, steam machine
look

## The 6 shots (reuse from the other carousel, or regenerate)

1. **Splash** — turmeric powder mid-fall into milk, frozen at impact, a
   crown-shaped splash thrown upward.
2. **First swirl** — overhead, powder just landed on the milk surface,
   blooming outward, most of the surface still white.
3. **The pour** — golden milk poured in a thin stream into a matte
   ceramic cup, a wisp of steam rising.
4. **Full marble** — extreme close-up, top-down, powder and milk
   mid-mix, dense marbled pattern filling the frame.
5. **Lifted** — a ceramic spoon lifts a thick ribbon of golden milk out
   of the cup, caught mid-air.
6. **Fully mixed** — extreme macro of the finished golden milk's smooth
   texture, tiny bubbles, no edges visible.
