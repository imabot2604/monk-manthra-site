# monk manthra — photo carousel (v2, centered)

A second carousel style, alongside the typographic one in
`../monk-manthra-intro/`. This one follows the reference format at
instagram.com/p/DdJ5o76kz9z/ — full-bleed macro photography with bold
**centered** headline text overlaid, closing on a pure gradient card.

**Locked for now: one motif only — yellow turmeric powder meeting milk.**
No product packets, pouches, or labels appear anywhere in this carousel;
that's deliberate, not a placeholder. Photography stays inside the
existing brand rule from [IMAGE-PROMPTS.md](../../IMAGE-PROMPTS.md): no
hands, no faces, no models, no props beyond the milk/powder/vessel
themselves.

7 slides. 6 are photographs you generate in Google Flow from the prompts
below; slide 7 is a pure gradient card with no photo, already built.

## How this works

1. Generate the 6 images below in Google Flow, one per prompt.
2. Send them back (any order, just say which slide each is).
3. I composite the centered headline text over each photo myself, in the
   same HTML/Chrome pipeline that built the last carousel — so the type
   stays crisp, on-brand (Fraunces/Karla, exact palette), and matches the
   pure-gradient slide 7 exactly. Flow only needs to produce the clean
   photographic background, no text baked in.

## Prompt scaffold

Hold this preamble constant across all 6 briefs, then append the
numbered scene below it.

> Macro photograph, shallow depth of field, dramatic single-source
> lighting from the upper left, deep shadow falling away into near-black.
> Subject: turmeric powder (vivid golden-yellow) and dairy milk (warm
> white), nothing else in frame. Matte surfaces, no gloss, no plastic, no
> packaging of any kind, no labels, no text, no logos, no hands, no
> models, no props, no plants, no scattered spices beyond the turmeric
> itself. Photographic, not a 3D render or illustration. Square, 1:1,
> high detail, editorial food-photography quality. Background is
> near-black or deep aubergine-purple, letting the gold and white read as
> the only bright colours in frame.

**Negative prompt for every shot:**

> hands, fingers, model, face, packaging, pouch, jar, label, text,
> watermark, signature, logo, gloss, plastic, gradient background,
> lens flare, multiple light sources, busy background, wood grain,
> marble, props, spoons other than where specified, steam machine look

## The 6 shots

### 1 · Cover — the splash

> [preamble] Turmeric powder is captured mid-fall, cascading downward
> into a shallow pool of milk below, frozen at the exact moment of
> impact — a crown-shaped splash of milk droplets thrown upward, powder
> still suspended in the air like a golden cloud. Extreme drama, high
> shutter-speed food-photography style.

### 2 · Just landed — the first swirl

> [preamble] Directly overhead shot, looking straight down into a wide
> shallow vessel of milk. A small mound of turmeric powder has just
> landed on the surface and is beginning to bloom outward into the milk
> in soft golden tendrils, most of the surface still white, the swirl
> just starting.

### 3 · The pour

> [preamble] Side-angle shot of milk — already fully golden — being
> poured in a thin steady stream from just above frame into a plain
> matte ceramic cup below, a wisp of steam curling up from the cup. The
> stream of liquid is the only warm light source; everything else falls
> into shadow.

### 4 · Full marble

> [preamble] Extreme close-up, top-down, of milk and turmeric mid-mix —
> not yet fully blended — golden and white swirled together in a dense
> marbled pattern, like a marbled-paper texture. No vessel edge visible,
> the swirl fills the entire frame.

### 5 · Lifted

> [preamble] A plain matte ceramic spoon lifts a thick pour of fully
> mixed golden milk up and out of a cup below, caught mid-air, a ribbon
> of golden liquid trailing and just starting to fall back down. Side
> lighting, the falling ribbon catching the light.

### 6 · Fully mixed

> [preamble] Extreme macro of the fully blended golden milk's surface —
> smooth, creamy, warm golden-white, a few tiny bubbles catching the
> light, filling the entire frame with no edges or vessel visible. The
> calmest, least dramatic shot of the six — texture and colour only.

## Copy for slides 1–6 (centered overlay, added after you send the photos)

1. "Turmeric deserves the credit. It just can't do the job alone."
2. Heading: "Why turmeric alone usually falls short" — "Curcumin is
   poorly absorbed by the body on its own. Most of a raw dose passes
   straight through. Heat and fat change how much actually gets used."
3. "Which is why golden milk was never just turmeric."
4. "But a ritual only works if you trust what's in the cup." — "The rest
   of the formula isn't there for colour. It's there to help the body
   actually use what you just drank."
5. "So the formula isn't a secret. It's a list."
6. "Every ingredient earns its place, or it doesn't make the scoop."
