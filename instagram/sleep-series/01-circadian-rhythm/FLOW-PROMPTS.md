# Sleep series, Post 1: Circadian Rhythm — image prompts

4 slides total. Slides 1-3 need a photo each (below); slide 4 is a plain
gradient card with no photo, built separately. Models/people are fine
here — this series isn't bound by the product-photography "no hands, no
models" rule.

## Shared scaffold

> Natural, candid lifestyle photography, shot on a full-frame camera
> with a 35mm or 50mm lens, soft available light (window or early
> morning sun), warm and calm colour grade — muted, not saturated,
> nothing that reads as a gym-ad or stock-photo energy. Real, relaxed
> human presence, not posed at the camera. Square, 1:1, shallow depth of
> field. Photographic realism, not illustration or 3D render.

**Negative prompt:** stock-photo smile, posed-at-camera, harsh flash,
oversaturated, gym-ad energy, text, watermark, logo, cartoon, CGI look

## The 3 shots

### 1 · Waking (cover slide)
> [scaffold] A person stretching or slowly waking up in bed, seen from
> behind or at a candid three-quarter angle — not facing the camera —
> soft morning light coming through a curtain, bedding in warm neutral
> tones.

### 2 · Dusk window
> [scaffold] A softly lit bedroom interior at dusk, window light fading
> to blue-gold, calm and still. Either empty, or a person silhouetted
> against the window, back to camera.

### 3 · Morning light
> [scaffold] Someone stepping toward an open window or door into morning
> sunlight, or standing by a window holding a warm drink, natural and
> unposed, soft light on their face or profile.
