Tired at 2pm. Wide awake at 11pm. Your clock isn't broken — it's just confused.

Every body runs on a roughly 24-hour internal clock — your circadian rhythm — that decides when you feel alert and when you feel sleepy. Nobody hands you the manual for it, and most of what throws it off is invisible: screens at midnight, waking up at a different time every day, spending your whole morning indoors.

Three things actually move it, and none of them are a supplement:

— Morning sunlight, within the first hour of waking
— A consistent wake time, even on weekends
— Dim light and no screens in the hour before bed

Rhythm first. Supplements second. Never the other way around.
