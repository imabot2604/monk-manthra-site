# Golden Milk ingredient carousels — captions

14 carousels, one per ingredient, 2 slides each (`png/NN-ingredient/`).
Ginger Extract has been dropped from the formula — see
[products/golden-milk.html](../../products/golden-milk.html), which now
lists 14 ingredients, not 15.

**Hook = pain point, pinned.** Slide 1's headline is the problem, not the
ingredient — the ingredient name is the reveal underneath it (small, gold,
after a short rule). Slide 2 is the why. Captions below follow the same
order: pain → reveal → why.

**Still no dosage anywhere** — the real per-serving amounts aren't
confirmed yet. Don't add a number to any of these until the formulation
is locked and the site's facts panel has been updated to match. Same
no-medical-claims discipline as the rest of the site
([INSTAGRAM.md](../../INSTAGRAM.md)).

### 01 — Turmeric Extract
The joints that complain before the weather even changes.
— Turmeric Extract

Turmeric is why golden milk is golden. We use an extract standardised for
curcuminoids instead of raw powder, and pair it with black pepper —
turmeric is poorly absorbed alone.

### 02 — Monk Fruit Extract
Wanting something sweet without paying for it at 2am.
— Monk Fruit Extract

A fruit extract sweetener stands in for sugar, so an evening drink doesn't
end in a spike right before bed. No added sugar. No artificial sweetener
either.

### 03 — Natural Vanilla
A supplement that tastes like one.
— Natural Vanilla

Vanilla rounds off the spice and the sweetener so the cup tastes finished —
closer to dessert than to medicine.

### 04 — Pumpkin Powder
The fibre nobody plans for and everybody needs.
— Pumpkin Powder

Pumpkin powder adds body and a mild natural sweetness, and brings its own
fibre along with it. Whole food, not an extract.

### 05 — Black Pepper Extract
Taking turmeric every day and absorbing almost none of it.
— Black Pepper Extract

Turmeric is poorly absorbed on its own. Piperine from black pepper is the
standard way to help the body take up the curcumin — which is why it's in
here at all.

### 06 — Cinnamon Extract
The spice everyone assumes is already in there.
— Cinnamon Extract

Cinnamon is one of the ingredients golden milk is named for. An extract
keeps the flavour without a large raw-powder dose.

### 07 — Nutmeg Extract
A blend that tastes almost right, but not quite.
— Nutmeg Extract

A touch of nutmeg rounds out the turmeric-cinnamon base — the extra spice
that makes it taste like the drink people already know.

### 08 — Taurine
Running on empty by 9pm and still wired.
— Taurine

One of the most-studied amino acids for everyday calm, included as a
standard part of the blend — not as a trend ingredient.

### 09 — MCT Powder
Wanting it creamy without reaching for dairy.
— MCT Powder

MCT powder gives the drink a creamy body even without dairy, and helps
carry curcumin, which is fat-soluble and needs something to travel with.

### 10 — Glycine
Tired all day, then lying there wide awake.
— Glycine

Glycine is the amino acid most associated with winding down before sleep —
taken in the evening, on purpose, for exactly this reason.

### 11 — Magnesium Glycinate
Wired all day. Wide awake at night.
— Magnesium Glycinate

A well-absorbed, gentle-on-the-stomach form of magnesium, bound to
glycine so one ingredient does two jobs in the same scoop.

### 12 — Lutein
Eyes worn out before the day is.
— Lutein

Lutein is a carotenoid the eye already stores for itself — usually found
in greens, and just as often skipped.

### 13 — Zeaxanthin
Screens all day, and nothing done about it.
— Zeaxanthin

Zeaxanthin works alongside lutein in the same eye tissue. The two are
almost always dosed together for a reason — this scoop doesn't split
them up.

### 14 — Fiber
The gram of fibre most days quietly skip.
— Fiber

A little fibre in a drink you're already taking daily. No claim needed —
no reason not to.
