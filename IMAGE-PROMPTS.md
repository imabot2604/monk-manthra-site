# monk manthra — image briefs

Everything here follows Section Eleven of the brand guide:

> Photography, when used: overhead, single object, soft north light, deep shadow,
> half-white or linen ground. No hands holding jars against sunsets.

And Section Eight, which governs what may appear on a pack:

> Front of pack carries four things only: mark, wordmark, state, ingredient.
> Nothing else. Dose and count sit at the base in mono.

## Two problems in the current set, worth a regeneration

1. **No product name on any label.** Every frame reads `monk manthra` only. The
   guide requires the state and the ingredient on the front of pack, and the dose
   and count in mono at the base. Right now the name only exists in the web
   typography beside the photo.
2. **Three frames carry `BRAND & VISUAL GUIDELINES` as the sub-line** — the pouch,
   the carton and the open-jar shot. That is the title of the PDF bleeding into
   the mockup. It is illegible at card size but wrong on the product pages.

## Prompt scaffold

Hold this preamble constant across every shot so the range stays one family, then
append one of the numbered briefs below.

> Overhead flat-lay product photograph, shot straight down. Single object,
> centred, generous empty space around it. Soft directional north light from the
> upper left, producing one long soft-edged shadow falling to the lower right.
> Ground is a natural undyed linen cloth, warm off-white, visible weave and a few
> soft creases. Muted, calm, editorial. Matte finishes throughout, no gloss, no
> reflections, no specular highlights. No hands, no props, no plants, no
> botanicals, no mortar and pestle, no scattered spices, no text overlays, no
> logos other than those described. Photographic, not a 3D render. Square,
> 1:1, high detail.

**Negative prompt for every shot:**

> gloss, varnish, shiny plastic, gradient, lens flare, sunset light, hands,
> model, leaves, herbs, botanical illustration, mortar and pestle, watermark,
> signature, sparkle, badge, seal, "clinically proven", starburst, price tag,
> multiple light sources, harsh shadow, busy background, wood grain, marble

## The pack, described once

Reuse this block verbatim inside each brief so the label is consistent.

> An amber glass supplement jar with a deep aubergine-purple screw cap. Wrapped
> around it is an uncoated cream paper label with a slightly textured, matte,
> letterpress feel. On the label, from top to bottom: a small gold circular mark
> made of a solid gold dot at the centre with two thin concentric purple rings
> and one thin gold outer ring around it, each ring broken by a small gap at the
> twelve o'clock position; below it the lowercase words "monk manthra" in a light
> serif with wide letter-spacing, in deep purple; a short thin gold horizontal
> rule; then the product name; then the ingredient line in small spaced capitals;
> and at the base of the label, the dose and capsule count in a monospaced face.

## Dropping new images in

Put the finished square frames anywhere, then:

```bash
python3 process-images.py ~/path/to/new-frames
```

That crops the watermark strip, writes `slug.jpg` and `slug@700.jpg` into
`assets/img/products/`, and reports whether the watermark actually cleared.
Then rebuild:

```bash
python3 build.py
```

Any product without a photograph falls back to the pack drawn to the Section
Eight spec, so the site is never broken by a missing file.
