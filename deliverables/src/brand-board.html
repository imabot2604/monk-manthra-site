01

The idea

A word, repeated

A manthra is one word, said again, until it changes something. A supplement is a small dose, taken daily, until the effect compounds. Every decision on this board traces back to that parallel — repetition over intensity, nothing shouts, white space is the product.

POSITIONING · STEADY, NOT SUPERCHARGED  ·  AUDIENCE · 28–45, READS THE PANEL

02

The mark

A gold seed, three rings travelling out, and a gap at the top where the sound escapes.

The seed radius is the unit; ring radii step at 1.6×, so spacing loosens outward the way a real ripple does. Every arc runs 20° to 340°, one stroke weight per ring, round caps. Gold appears exactly twice — the seed and the outer ring.

Full colour on half white
Reversed on deep purple
One colour on gold

Reduction · the mark drops rings as it shrinks

≥ 96px · THREE RINGS

48–95px · TWO

≤ 32px · ONE + SEED

Dashed box = clear space: one seed diameter on all four sides, twice that on packaging faces. Nothing enters it — no type, no rules, no product photography, no retailer badges. Minimum sizes: print 12mm wide · screen 32px wide · foil or emboss 16mm wide. Never close the gap, never rotate, never stretch, never put type inside the rings.

Typography

Steady, not supercharged

Fraunces Light 200–300 · TRACKING .13–.16em

Wordmark, product states, headlines. Lowercase. The soft serifs keep it human — a Cormorant or Playfair would put us on the same shelf as every other wellness label. Never bold a headline; if it needs emphasis, give it space instead.

07

Product architecture

Named for the state it supports. Verified by the ingredient below it.

The customer shops by feeling and checks by ingredient — never the other way round. One word only; if a product needs two, it needs a rethink. Products are separated by a 4mm tonal band drawn from the core palette, not by new colours, so the shelf stays one family. Never number the range — numbers belong to doses.

12

Voice & claims

The calmest voice in a loud category.

Plain verbs, no exclamation marks, no urgency. That restraint is the entire differentiation.

Write this

Take one capsule with the evening meal.

Not this

Unlock your best sleep ever!

Write this

600mg of ashwagandha. Nothing else added.

Not this

Our proprietary blend of ancient botanicals.

Write this

Most people notice something by week three.

Not this

Feel the difference from day one — guaranteed.

Claims discipline

Never say a product treats, prevents, cures or diagnoses anything. Structure-and-function phrasing only — "supports", "contributes to" — and only at the dose actually sold. Carry the market's required disclaimer on every pack and product page; in India that means the FSSAI nutraceutical declarations, which this brand does not yet hold. No before-and-after imagery, no weight-loss framing, no "clinically proven". This board is design guidance, not legal advice — have a regulatory consultant sign off every pack and claim before print.

11

Social & motion

The grid reads as one continuous surface — dark, light, gold, repeat — so the feed itself becomes the ripple. Photography, where used: overhead, single object, soft north light, deep shadow, half-white or linen ground. No hands holding jars against sunsets. Motion: the ripple draws outward over 1.4s, once, on load. It never loops and never pulses. The mark at the top of this board has already done it.
