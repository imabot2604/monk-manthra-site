Clear space on signage

The guide fixes clear space at one seed diameter, and twice that on packaging faces. At a 44 mm mark the seed is Ø 6.2 mm, so the packaging rule gives 12.4 mm — too tight for a plate a person stands in front of. The 24 mm margin here is roughly four seed diameters and is an extension of the rule, not something the guide states. Hold it as the minimum, not the target.

C

Finish

Gold is foil or metal. Never a printed gradient.

Section five holds on a wall exactly as it holds on a page: purple carries the board, gold marks the one thing you want noticed. On the purple plate the gold is the outer ring, the seed and the hairline — about 0.5% of the face, against a ceiling of ten.

Why standoffs

The board floats 20 mm off the wall and throws its own shadow. That is the same instruction the guide gives photography — single object, soft light, deep shadow — carried onto the building. A flush-glued plate loses it.

E

Never

✕

Never close the gap at the top of the rings to fit a tighter board.

✕

No backlit or edge-lit acrylic. A glowing purple field breaks 60/30/10 and the mark stops being a mark.

✕

No gold gradient, no mirror-chrome, no rope light. Flat metal or flat vinyl only.

✕

Never stretch the board to a wall's proportion. Change the size, hold 3:2 or 3:1.

✕

No phone number, no QR code, no shutter timings on the plate. The address is all it carries.

✕

Never drop below 16 mm mark width — the guide's foil and emboss minimum.

Before you order

Confirm the registered name and address against the company filing — the board is the one place a wrong line is expensive. The plate carries no phone, no email and no licence number by design; if a municipal or FSSAI display requirement applies at this address, it goes on a separate compliance plate, never on this one.
